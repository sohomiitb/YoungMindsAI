About Me

Hello! I'm Sohom Mandal, an experienced AI educator and data science professional with a strong academic and industry background. I am an IIT Bombay alumnus with a PhD in Civil Engineering and currently pursuing my MBA at the Rotman School of Management, University of Toronto. I am also a Data Analytics Professor at Northeastern University. With over decades of experience in teaching and industry, I blend advanced academic expertise with engaging teaching methods to make AI and coding fun, practical, and inspiring for students from Grade 7 to 12. My mission is to help young minds unlock their creativity and build confidence in this AI-driven world — transforming curiosity into real-world skills and innovative projects.
